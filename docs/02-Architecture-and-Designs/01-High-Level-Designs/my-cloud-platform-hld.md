# 2. Executive Summary

## 2.1 Overview

My Cloud Services is a greenfield cloud platform designed to deliver AI, data, Kubernetes, networking, and observability services to enterprise customers. It leverages VMware Aria Suite, NSX‑T, and Tanzu Kubernetes Grid to provide a secure, scalable, and highly available environment.

## 2.2 Business Drivers

- Digital transformation
- Cost optimization
- Platform modernization
- Security improvement
- Service consolidation
- Cloud migration

## 2.3 Goals & Objectives

### Business Objectives

- Reduce operational costs
- Improve customer experience
- Accelerate time to market

### Technical Objectives

- Improve availability
- Enhance scalability
- Increase automation
- Strengthen resiliency

## 2.4 Scope

### In Scope

- AI Platform
- Data Platform
- Kubernetes Platform
- Networking Foundation
- Observability Services
- Automation and CI/CD pipelines

### Out of Scope

- Legacy system integration
- Future roadmap features beyond the initial release
- Unsupported platforms
